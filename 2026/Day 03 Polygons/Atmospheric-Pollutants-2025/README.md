# Atmospheric Pollutants in Uzbekistan (2025)


## About the Map

Day 03. Polygons

The map shows the annual volume of atmospheric pollutants released from stationary sources across Uzbekistan in 2025.

The statistics are reported in thousand tonnes and represent emissions from stationary sources, such as industrial facilities and other fixed sources. They describe the amount of pollutants released into the atmosphere, rather than ambient air pollution concentrations or overall air quality.

## Key Finding

Uzbekistan's reported national total increased from **729.0 thousand tonnes in 2010 to 1,188.2 thousand tonnes in 2025**, representing an increase of approximately 63%.

This upward trend may be associated with industrial development and increasing economic activity. However, further analysis would be needed to identify the main factors behind this change.
