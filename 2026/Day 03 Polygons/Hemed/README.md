# Uzbekistan Regional Population

**Author:** Hemed Lungo (GitHub: [Heed725](https://github.com/Heed725))  
**Data:** National Statistics Committee of Uzbekistan, population on 1 January 2026; geoBoundaries gbOpen admin-1 boundaries, representing 2017, sourced from OpenStreetMap / Wambacher.  
**Tools:** QGIS, PyQGIS and Barlow typography.

This four-column facet map compares the population of Uzbekistan's 14 first-level administrative areas: 12 regions, the Republic of Karakalpakstan and Tashkent city. Areas are ranked from highest to lowest population, with darker teal indicating a larger population and each name and population shown below its outline.

Outlines are resized to fit equal cells, so their size does not represent population or geographic area. Population figures are rounded to the nearest 100 people; boundary geometry represents 2017.
