# Day 16: Collaborative map

**Date:** 16 November 2026

## Theme
Do a map with a friend, colleague or a stranger. AI doesn't count as a friend :)

## 📘 Tutorial
Do a map with a stranger! Follow the [Day 16 tutorial](tutorial/README.md) to explore 107,288 Mapillary street photos of Tashkent taken by 47 people, map the city one colour per person in Google Colab, look at their photos through the Mapillary API, and then walk your own streets with friends and add them to the map. Screenshots and PDFs in English, Russian and Uzbek.
