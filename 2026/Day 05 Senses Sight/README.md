# Day 05: Senses: Sight

**Date:** 05 November 2026

## Theme
How do you see something? Maybe map a viewshed?

## 📘 Tutorial
Your first steps in the terminal: follow the [Day 05 tutorial](tutorial/README.md) to find out what you can see from the Tashkent TV Tower and from Big Chimgan. You download Copernicus satellite elevation data, build a hillshade and viewsheds with GDAL, measure the visible area, and put the result on Sentinel-2 imagery in QGIS. Step-by-step screenshots and PDFs in English, Russian and Uzbek.
