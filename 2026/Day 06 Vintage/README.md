# Day 06: Vintage

**Date:** 06 November 2026

## Theme
Spin up your time machine and do a map which looks like a historical map, or maybe the theme itself should be vintage.

## 📘 Tutorial
Put a real 1913 map of Tashkent on today's city: follow the [Day 06 tutorial](tutorial/README.md). You georeference Pirogovsky's 1913 city plan in your browser with Allmaps (no installation, no account), compare it with Sentinel-2 satellite images and OpenStreetMap, and make a vintage swipe map. Step-by-step screenshots and PDFs in English, Russian and Uzbek.
