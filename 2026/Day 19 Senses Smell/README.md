# Day 19: Senses: Smell

**Date:** 19 November 2026

## Theme
How do you map things which your nose can sense? What does a smelly map look like?

## 📘 Tutorial
What does a smelly map look like? Follow the [Day 19 tutorial](tutorial/README.md) to map the gases you can smell, NO₂ (car exhaust) and SO₂ (burnt match), with Sentinel-5P in Google Earth Engine: rank the cities, follow a plume on one winter day, compare winter and summer, and click the map to smell a place.
