# Day 25: Is this a map?

**Date:** 25 November 2026

## Theme
Stretch the limits of maps and blend your map with an artwork or some creative data visualization. When does something become a map and when does it stop being one?

## 📘 Tutorial
Is a 3D picture still a map? Follow the [Day 25 tutorial](tutorial/README.md) to turn the heights of Chimgan and Charvak into a 3D map with plotly in Google Colab: rotate it with your mouse, see what vertical exaggeration does, cut a cake slice of the mountains, lay a Sentinel-2 picture on top, save it as a web page, and draw the same mountains with lines only. Screenshots and PDFs in English, Russian and Uzbek.
