# Day 23: Senses: Taste

**Date:** 23 November 2026

## Theme
Food, drink or something else which you can taste.

## 📘 Tutorial
You can't taste water from space, but you can see the mud in it. Follow the [Day 23 tutorial](tutorial/README.md) to map the Charvak reservoir, where much of Tashkent's water comes from, with Sentinel-2 in Google Colab: find the water, measure its area and how muddy it is, see how the reservoir settles the mud, and follow it from spring to autumn. Screenshots and PDFs in English, Russian and Uzbek.
