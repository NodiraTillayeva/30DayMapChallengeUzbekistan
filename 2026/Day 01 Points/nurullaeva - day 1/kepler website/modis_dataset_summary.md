# MODIS Active Fire Dataset Summary (Uzbekistan)

* **Location:** Uzbekistan (approx. $37.2^\circ\text{N} - 44.0^\circ\text{N}$, $58.6^\circ\text{E} - 73.0^\circ\text{E}$), covering the period from **January 4, 2025, to May 17, 2025**.
* **What the Points Indicate:** Each point represents a $1\text{ km}$ spatial pixel where NASA's **MODIS** sensor (on Terra or Aqua satellites) detected a thermal anomaly (hotspot, open burning, or fire radiative power).

### Key Indicators & Values

* **Thermal Intensity / Brightness ($K$):** Measures channel brightness temperature in Kelvin. Higher values indicate hotter surface thermal anomalies.
* **Fire Radiative Power (FRP):** Measured in Megawatts ($\text{MW}$), indicating the rate of heat energy released by the fire/hotspot.
* **Confidence Score (%):** A percentage ($0\% - 100\%$) indicating the detection reliability of the thermal anomaly.
* **Fire Type Classification:**
  * `0`: Presumed active vegetation / wildfire.
  * `2`: Static land source or industrial thermal activity.
* **Day/Night (`D`/`N`):** Indicates whether the satellite observation occurred during daylight or nighttime overpasses.
