# Fire Stations in Uzbekistan


## About the Map

This map shows the spatial distribution of fire stations across Uzbekistan, with a closer look at their locations in Tashkent. It helps visualize the geographic distribution of emergency response infrastructure.

## Data Sources
- OpenStreetMap (OSM)

## Tools
- ArcGIS online

## Files
- `Fire_stations_UZB.jpg` – Final map
- `fire_stations_UZB.geojson` – Fire station locations across Uzbekistan
- `fire_stations_Tashkent.geojson` – Fire station locations in Tashkent
- `Tashkent_boundary.geojson` – Tashkent administrative boundary
